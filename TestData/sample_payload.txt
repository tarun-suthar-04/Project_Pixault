# Pixault Testing Payload
# Purpose: Validation of AES-256-GCM and LSB Steganography Integrity

This is a sample secure payload used to test the capacity and integrity of the Pixault steganography engine.
The system must be able to:
1. Encrypt this text using a password-derived key (Argon2id).
2. Embed the ciphertext into the Least Significant Bits of a carrier image.
3. Reconstruct the bit-map and decrypt the message without a single bit of loss.

PAYLOAD DATA:
"The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. Security is not a product, but a process. 
Zero-Trust Architecture is the foundation of the Pixault Secure Vault."
